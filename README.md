# 📊 AtliQ Hardware - Ad-Hoc Insights in Consumer Goods

Welcome to the **Ad-Hoc Insights Case Study** for AtliQ Hardware! This project focuses on analyzing top-level management's strategic business requests using SQL, Excel, Power BI, and data presentation techniques in the consumer goods domain.

---

## 🎯 Project Overview

AtliQ Hardware, a leading consumer electronics manufacturer, required data-driven insights to evaluate market presence, sales performance, product segment growth, and customer discount structures across fiscal years 2020 and 2021. 

This repository contains the structured SQL queries, data visualizations, executive presentation deck, and key insights generated to address 10 strategic ad-hoc requests from executive leadership.

## 📊 Key Ad-Hoc Requests & Insights Summary

1. **Market Footprint:** Identified APAC markets where "Atliq Exclusive" operates its business.
2. **Product Growth (2020 vs 2021):** Calculated the percentage increase in unique products offered year-over-year.
3. **Segment Distribution:** Ranked unique product counts across all product segments (Accessories, Notebooks, Peripherals, etc.).
4. **Segment Growth Analysis:** Determined which product segments experienced the highest growth in unique products from FY 2020 to FY 2021.
5. **Manufacturing Costs:** Highlighted the highest and lowest manufacturing cost products.
6. **Top Discount Customers (FY 2021):** Evaluated the top 5 Indian market customers receiving the highest average pre-invoice discount percentage.
7. **Monthly Sales Performance:** Analyzed monthly gross sales trends for "Atliq Exclusive" to identify peak demand seasons.
8. **Quarterly Sales Breakdown:** Evaluated total sold quantities per quarter in FY 2020 to pinpoint top-performing quarters.
9. **Channel Contribution (FY 2021):** Evaluated gross sales contribution and percentage distribution across channels (*Retailer*, *Direct*, *Distributor*).
10. **Top Products by Division:** Ranked the top 3 products in each division (*P&A*, *N&P*, *PC*) by total quantity sold in FY 2021.
